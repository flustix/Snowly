# Privacy Policy for Snowly

## Information We Collect
Depending on how you use this bot, we may collect the following data:
*   **Discord User Data:** Your Discord User ID and username.
*   **Server Data:** Guild IDs, channel IDs, and role IDs necessary for configuration.
*   **Configuration:** Any data provided for configuring features of the bot.

## How We Use Your Information
We use the collected information solely for the following purposes:
*   To provide, operate, and maintain the bot's features.
*   To save user preferences and server settings.

## Data Storage and Security
We take reasonable security measures to protect your data. Stored data is only accessible to the developer.

## Data Sharing
We **do not** sell, trade, or otherwise transfer your personally identifiable information to outside parties. 

## Data Deletion
You can request the deletion of your data at any time by contacting us directly at me@flux.moe. Upon request, we will purge your data from our database.
When requesting deletion, please provide the necessary guild/user IDs. 

## Changes to This Policy
We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Continued use of the bot after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
